### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Subscription revenue leakage currently poses a significant risk to recurring revenue. Across our active customer base of **7,043 accounts**, our overall churn rate stands at **26.54%** (1,869 lost customers), representing **$139,130.85 in Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) at risk**, or **$1,669,570.20 in Annualized Recurring Revenue (ARR)**.

Our empirical churn analysis reveals that revenue loss is heavily concentrated within three specific operational segments. By executing a targeted retention program focused on our highest-risk segment (Month-to-Month contract customers):

> **Reducing Month-to-Month customer churn by just 10% will protect $136,058.94 in Annualized Recurring Revenue (ARR) across 166 retained customer accounts.**

---

### TOP 3 BUSINESS CHURN DRIVERS

Statistical driver evaluation (Chi-Square test of independence and Cramér’s V association ranking) identifies three primary root causes driving customer attrition:

| Customer Attribute | Churn Rate (%) | Baseline MRR Risk | Association Strength |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1. Month-to-Month Contracts** | 42.71% | $113,382.45 / mo | Very Strong ($V = 0.41$) |
| **2. Electronic Check Payment** | 45.29% | $79,271.65 / mo | Strong ($V = 0.30$) |
| **3. Fiber Optic Internet Service** | 41.89% | $107,762.60 / mo | Strong ($V = 0.32$) |

#### 1. Contract Commitment Gap (Month-to-Month Contracts)
* **The Data:** Customers on Month-to-month contracts experience a **42.71% churn rate**, compared to **11.27%** for 1-year contracts and **2.83%** for 2-year contracts.
* **Business Reality:** Month-to-month customers exhibit a **15.1x higher churn multiplier** relative to 2-year contract holders. This single cohort accounts for **88.5% of total churned customers** (1,655 out of 1,869 lost accounts) and **$1.36M in annual revenue at risk**.

#### 2. Payment Channel Friction (Electronic Check Users)
* **The Data:** Customers paying via Electronic Check churn at **45.29%**, compared to **15.24%–16.71%** for automated payment options (Credit Card / Bank Transfer).
* **Business Reality:** Manual payment channels suffer from **3.0x higher churn** than automated billing. Electronic check payments create monthly transaction friction, exposing customers to recurring cancellation decisions.

#### 3. Fiber Optic Product & Price Disconnect
* **The Data:** Fiber Optic subscribers churn at **41.89%** (vs. 18.96% for DSL), despite paying premium monthly rates ($80–$110/month).
* **Business Reality:** Fiber Optic accounts represent **$1.29M in annual revenue at risk**. High monthly bills combined with low adoption of retention add-on services (OnlineSecurity, TechSupport) lead to early dissatisfaction during the first 12 months of tenure.

### RECOMMENDED RETENTION ACTIONS

To capture the **$136K+ annual revenue protection opportunity**, we recommend three immediate operational initiatives tied directly to these drivers:

| Recommended Initiative | Target Cohort | Projected Annualized Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Contract Migration Incentive** | Month-to-Month (0–12 Mos Tenure) | Protects **$136,058.94 ARR** |
| **2. Autopay Conversion Credit** | Electronic Check Users | Retains **~200 accounts ($165K ARR)** |
| **3. Fiber Optic Service Bundle** | New Fiber Optic Onboardings | Reduces early churn by **15–20%** |

#### Action 1: First-Year Contract Migration Campaign
* **Execution:** Launch an automated targeted campaign offering a **$10/month bill discount** for Month-to-month customers in their first 12 months who commit to a 12-month or 24-month agreement.
* **Expected Impact:** Achieving a 10% conversion rate converts 166 high-risk accounts into stable multi-year agreements, securing **$136,058.94 in ARR**.

#### Action 2: Autopay Conversion ($10 One-Time Bill Credit)
* **Execution:** Offer a **one-time $10 statement credit** to Electronic Check users who enroll in recurring Credit Card or Bank Transfer Auto-Pay.
* **Expected Impact:** Shifting customers from manual to automated payments reduces friction and historical churn rates by over 60% (from 45.3% down to ~16%).

#### Action 3: Complimentary Fiber Optic Care Bundle
* **Execution:** Automatically bundle **6 months of free TechSupport & OnlineSecurity** with all new Fiber Optic activations.
* **Expected Impact:** Increasing initial service value perception and technical assistance during onboarding directly targets the root cause of early-tenure Fiber Optic cancellations.
